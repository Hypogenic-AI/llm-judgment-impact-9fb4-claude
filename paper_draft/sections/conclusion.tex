\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether judgmental tone in user prompts can make LLMs think better, longer, or perhaps worse. The answer across $5{,}100$ controlled API calls on two frontier OpenAI models, three datasets, and a six-level preemptive tone ladder is: \textbf{yes longer, slightly better, and worse in a specific multi-turn way}.

Models think \emph{longer} when judged --- completion tokens grow by $19$--$57\%$ from neutral to ``I'm going to grade this'' on \tqa, significant at $p \le 10^{-9}$ on paired Wilcoxon for both models. They think \emph{slightly better} only on the dataset with headroom: on \tqa a small $+3$--$+4$\,pp accuracy lift appears under every skeptic level for \gptfomini, with all four item-level disagreements between neutral and the skeptic majority falling in favour of the skeptic (one-sided exact $p=0.0625$). \gsm and \mmlu sit at ceiling and show no effect. They think \emph{worse} in the carry-over: post-rebuttal correct$\rightarrow$wrong flips on \tqa triple under preemptive judgmental tone, from $4\%$ at neutral to $13\%$ at ``I'm going to check your work carefully.''

Two surprising findings emerged. First, the verbal apology marker that the sycophancy literature uses as the operational definition of capitulation~\citep{laban2023flipflop} is \emph{extinct} in modern OpenAI models --- $0/5{,}100$ responses contain any apology keyword, including under hostile preemptive tone and under follow-up rebuttal. Capitulation is now silent. Second, priming with preemptive skepticism does not confer downstream rebuttal-resistance --- it amplifies fragility. The same tone region that lifts single-turn accuracy on \tqa is the region where \flipflop carry-over harm is largest.

\para{Practical takeaway.} Mild-to-strong preemptive skeptical tone is a small positive accuracy lever and a clear positive deliberation lever for one-shot, ambiguous-truth queries. In multi-turn settings, especially agent loops where the model may face follow-up challenges, the same tone amplifies destabilisation and should be either avoided or paired with explicit ``defend your answer'' guidance.

\para{Future work.} We see four immediate extensions. (i) A larger-scale \tqa replication (at $n\ge 500$ per cell) to push the $+3$\,pp sweet-spot signal cleanly into or out of statistical significance. (ii) Cross-provider replication on Claude, Gemini, and open-source models to test the family-direction differences predicted by \citet{rabbani2025factjudgment}. (iii) A reasoning-mode (\textsf{o}-series, R1) sweep that uses the visible CoT to distinguish ``more reasoning steps'' from ``more polished prose,'' per the rationalisation concern of \citet{feng2026goodarguments}. (iv) Replacement of the \psorry marker with a behavioural flip-detection metric for modern models, since the verbal apology pattern is no longer informative. The sweet spot exists; the next question is whether it remains a sweet spot in any task and setting outside the one our experiments lived in.
